### **Question 1**
* **Problem:** Complete the sentence below: A(n) _______ is the collection of all points in the plane, the sum of whose distances from two fixed points is a constant.
* **Answer:** **`ellipse`**
* **The Rule:** By geometric definition, an ellipse is the set of all points $(x, y)$ in a plane such that the sum of the distances from $(x, y)$ to two fixed points (the foci) is constant.
* **Work:**
  1. Recall the conic section definitions: a circle is equidistant from one center, a parabola is equidistant from a focus and directrix, a hyperbola has a constant *difference* of distances from two foci, and an **ellipse** has a constant *sum* of distances from two foci.
  2. Therefore, the blank is filled by **ellipse**.

### **Question 2**
* **Problem:** Explain how to determine the center of the ellipse $\frac{(x+3)^2}{9} + \frac{(y+5)^2}{4} = 1$.
* **Answer:** **Choice B** (`The ellipse equation is (x-h)^2/a^2 + (y-k)^2/b^2 = 1. The center is at (h,k). In this case, the center is (-3,-5).`)
* **Graph Equation:** `(x+3)^2/9 + (y+5)^2/4 = 1`
* **The Rule:** The standard form of an ellipse centered at $(h, k)$ is $\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1$.
* **Work:**
  1. Rewrite $(x+3)^2$ as $(x - (-3))^2$ and $(y+5)^2$ as $(y - (-5))^2$.
  2. Comparing to $(x-h)^2$ and $(y-k)^2$, we identify $h = -3$ and $k = -5$.
  3. Thus, the center $(h, k)$ is $(-3, -5)$, which corresponds to **Choice B**.

### **Question 3**
* **Problem:** Fill in the blank so that the resulting statement is true: The graph of $\frac{(x+2)^2}{9} + \frac{(y-5)^2}{4} = 1$ has its center at _______.
* **Answer:** **`(-2,5)`**
* **Graph Equation:** `(x+2)^2/9 + (y-5)^2/4 = 1`
* **The Rule:** For an ellipse in the form $\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1$, the center is the ordered pair $(h, k)$.
* **Work:**
  1. From $(x+2)^2 = (x - (-2))^2$, we get $h = -2$.
  2. From $(y-5)^2$, we get $k = 5$.
  3. Combining these gives the center $(-2, 5)$.

### **Question 4**
* **Problem:** Graph the ellipse and label the intercepts: $\frac{x^2}{81} + \frac{y^2}{36} = 1$.
* **Answer:** **Choice C** (Horizontal ellipse centered at $(0,0)$ with $x$-intercepts $(-9,0), (9,0)$ and $y$-intercepts $(0,6), (0,-6)$)
* **Graph Equation:** `x^2/81 + y^2/36 = 1`
* **The Rule:** For $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ centered at the origin, the $x$-intercepts are $(\pm a, 0)$ and the $y$-intercepts are $(0, \pm b)$.
* **Work:**
  1. Set $y = 0$ to find $x$-intercepts: $\frac{x^2}{81} = 1 \implies x^2 = 81 \implies x = \pm 9 \implies (-9, 0)$ and $(9, 0)$.
  2. Set $x = 0$ to find $y$-intercepts: $\frac{y^2}{36} = 1 \implies y^2 = 36 \implies y = \pm 6 \implies (0, -6)$ and $(0, 6)$.
  3. Match these intercepts to **Choice C**.

### **Question 6**
* **Problem:** Find the standard form of the equation for the ellipse centered at $(0,0)$, having vertical major axis of length $10$ and minor axis of length $6$. Graph the equation.
* **Answer:**
  * **Denominators:** **`9`** (under $x^2$) and **`25`** (under $y^2$), giving $\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{25} = 1$
  * **Graph:** **Choice D**
* **Graph Equation:** `x^2/9 + y^2/25 = 1`
* **The Rule:** A vertical major axis of length $2a$ places $a^2$ under $y^2$, and a horizontal minor axis of length $2b$ places $b^2$ under $x^2$.
* **Work:**
  1. Major axis is vertical with length $2a = 10 \implies a = 5 \implies a^2 = 25$ (under $y^2$).
  2. Minor axis is horizontal with length $2b = 6 \implies b = 3 \implies b^2 = 9$ (under $x^2$).
  3. Standard form equation: $\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{25} = 1$.
  4. The graph extends horizontally from $-3$ to $3$ and vertically from $-5$ to $5$ on a $[-10, 10]$ grid, which matches **Choice D**.

### **Question 7**
* **Problem:** The graph of an ellipse is given (extending horizontally from $-3$ to $3$ and vertically from $-5$ to $5$). Match the graph to its equation.
* **Answer:** **Choice D** (`x^2/9 + y^2/25 = 1`)
* **Graph Equation:** `x^2/9 + y^2/25 = 1`
* **The Rule:** If an ellipse centered at $(0,0)$ has $x$-intercepts $(\pm b, 0)$ and $y$-intercepts $(0, \pm a)$, its equation is $\frac{x^2}{b^2} + \frac{y^2}{a^2} = 1$.
* **Work:**
  1. Read the intercepts from the graph: $x$-intercepts at $(\pm 3, 0)$ and $y$-intercepts at $(0, \pm 5)$.
  2. Square the semi-axes: $b^2 = 3^2 = 9$ (under $x^2$) and $a^2 = 5^2 = 25$ (under $y^2$).
  3. The equation is $\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{25} = 1$, which is **Choice D**.

### **Question 8**
* **Problem:** The graph of an ellipse is given (extending horizontally from $-1$ to $1$ and vertically from $-9$ to $9$). Match the graph to its equation.
* **Answer:** **Choice B** (`x^2 + y^2/81 = 1`)
* **Graph Equation:** `x^2/1 + y^2/81 = 1`
* **The Rule:** An ellipse with $x$-intercepts $(\pm 1, 0)$ and $y$-intercepts $(0, \pm 9)$ has $b^2 = 1$ and $a^2 = 81$.
* **Work:**
  1. From the graph, the horizontal semi-axis is $b = 1 \implies b^2 = 1$.
  2. The vertical semi-axis is $a = 9 \implies a^2 = 81$.
  3. Substituting into $\frac{x^2}{b^2} + \frac{y^2}{a^2} = 1$ gives $x^2 + \frac{y^2}{81} = 1$ (**Choice B**).

### **Question 9**
* **Problem:** Find an equation for the ellipse whose graph is shown below (centered at $(0,0)$ with intercepts labeled at $(4,0)$ and $(0,5)$).
* **Answer:** **`x^2/16 + y^2/25 = 1`**
* **Graph Equation:** `x^2/16 + y^2/25 = 1`
* **The Rule:** Substitute the $x$-intercept $(b, 0)$ and $y$-intercept $(0, a)$ into $\frac{x^2}{b^2} + \frac{y^2}{a^2} = 1$.
* **Work:**
  1. The $x$-intercept is $(4, 0)$, so the denominator under $x^2$ is $4^2 = 16$.
  2. The $y$-intercept is $(0, 5)$, so the denominator under $y^2$ is $5^2 = 25$.
  3. Thus, the standard form equation is $\frac{x^2}{16} + \frac{y^2}{25} = 1$.

### **Question 10**
* **Problem:** Complete the sentence: For the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{25} = 1$, the vertices are the points (1) ______ and (2) ______.
* **Answer:**
  * **(1):** **`(0,-5)`**
  * **(2):** **`(0,5)`**
* **Graph Equation:** `x^2/4 + y^2/25 = 1`
* **The Rule:** The vertices of an ellipse lie on the major axis (the axis corresponding to the larger denominator $a^2$).
* **Work:**
  1. Compare denominators: $25 > 4$, and $25$ is under $y^2$, so the major axis is along the $y$-axis.
  2. Since $a^2 = 25 \implies a = 5$, the vertices are at $(0, -a)$ and $(0, a)$, which are $(0, -5)$ and $(0, 5)$.

### **Question 11**
* **Problem:** Write in standard form the equation of an ellipse with center at the origin, an $x$-intercept at $(3,0)$, and a $y$-intercept at $(0,-9)$.
* **Answer:** **`x^2/9 + y^2/81 = 1`**
* **Graph Equation:** `x^2/9 + y^2/81 = 1`
* **The Rule:** For an ellipse centered at $(0,0)$, the denominator under $x^2$ is the square of the $x$-intercept, and the denominator under $y^2$ is the square of the $y$-intercept.
* **Work:**
  1. Horizontal semi-axis: $b = 3 \implies b^2 = 9$.
  2. Vertical semi-axis: $a = |-9| = 9 \implies a^2 = 81$.
  3. Equation: $\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{81} = 1$.

### **Question 16**
* **Problem:** Write an equation for the graph (horizontal ellipse centered at $(-2,3)$ extending from $x = -5$ to $1$ and $y = 1$ to $5$).
* **Answer:** **Choice C** (`(x+2)^2/9 + (y-3)^2/4 = 1`)
* **Graph Equation:** `(x+2)^2/9 + (y-3)^2/4 = 1`
* **The Rule:** Find $(h, k)$ from the center, $a$ from the horizontal distance to the right/left vertex, and $b$ from the vertical distance to the top/bottom co-vertex.
* **Work:**
  1. The center is labeled $(h, k) = (-2, 3)$, so the numerators are $(x+2)^2$ and $(y-3)^2$.
  2. The horizontal distance from $(-2, 3)$ to $(1, 3)$ is $a = 3 \implies a^2 = 9$ (under $(x+2)^2$).
  3. The vertical distance from $(-2, 3)$ to $(-2, 5)$ is $b = 2 \implies b^2 = 4$ (under $(y-3)^2$).
  4. Equation: $\frac{(x+2)^2}{9} + \frac{(y-3)^2}{4} = 1$ (**Choice C**).
