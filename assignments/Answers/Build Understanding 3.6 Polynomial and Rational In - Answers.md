### **Question 1**
* **Problem:** Solve the polynomial inequality. Express the solution in interval notation: $(x - 9)(x + 5) > 0$.
* **Answer:** **Choice C (`(-∞, -5) U (9, ∞)`)**
* **Graph Equation:** `(x - 9)(x + 5) > 0`
* **The Rule:** A quadratic product $(x - a)(x - b) > 0$ (with $a < b$) is positive outside its roots, on $(-\infty, a) \cup (b, \infty)$.
* **Work:**
  1. Find boundary points (roots) by setting $(x - 9)(x + 5) = 0$:
     * $x - 9 = 0 \implies x = 9$
     * $x + 5 = 0 \implies x = -5$
  2. Sign chart across intervals $(-\infty, -5)$, $(-5, 9)$, and $(9, \infty)$:
     * Test $x = -6 \in (-\infty, -5)$: $(-6 - 9)(-6 + 5) = (-15)(-1) = +15 > 0$ (**Positive**)
     * Test $x = 0 \in (-5, 9)$: $(0 - 9)(0 + 5) = (-9)(5) = -45 < 0$ (**Negative**)
     * Test $x = 10 \in (9, \infty)$: $(10 - 9)(10 + 5) = (1)(15) = +15 > 0$ (**Positive**)
  3. Select positive intervals with open parentheses (strict inequality $> 0$): `(-∞, -5) U (9, ∞)` (**Choice C**).

### **Question 17**
* **Problem:** A baseball is hit so that its height, $s$, in feet after $t$ seconds is $s = -16t^2 + 36t + 5$. For what time period is the ball at least $25\text{ ft}$ above the ground?
* **Answer:** **Choice B: For the time period between (and inclusive of) `1` sec and `1.25` sec the ball will be at least 25 ft above the ground.**
* **Graph Equation:** `-16t^2 + 36t + 5 >= 25`
* **The Rule:** "At least $25\text{ ft}$" translates to the non-strict inequality $s(t) \ge 25$, which includes the endpoints of the time interval.
* **Work:**
  1. Set up the inequality:
     * $-16t^2 + 36t + 5 \ge 25$
  2. Subtract $25$ from both sides:
     * $-16t^2 + 36t - 20 \ge 0$
  3. Divide both sides by $-4$ and reverse the inequality sign:
     * $4t^2 - 9t + 5 \le 0$
  4. Factor the quadratic expression:
     * $(4t - 5)(t - 1) \le 0$
  5. Find the boundary times:
     * $t - 1 = 0 \implies t = 1\text{ sec}$
     * $4t - 5 = 0 \implies t = \frac{5}{4} = 1.25\text{ sec}$
  6. Since $(4t - 5)(t - 1) \le 0$ holds on $[1, 1.25]$, the ball is at least $25\text{ ft}$ above the ground between (and inclusive of) **`1`** sec and **`1.25`** sec (**Choice B**).
