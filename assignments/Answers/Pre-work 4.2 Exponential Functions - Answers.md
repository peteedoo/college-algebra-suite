### **Question 1**
* **Problem:** Decide whether the following statement is true or false. If it is false, explain why: $4^2 = 8$.
* **Answer:** **`Choice A: The statement 4^2 = 8 is false because the left side evaluates to` `16` `, which is not equal to the right side.`**
* **The Rule:** $b^2 = b \cdot b$ (multiply the base by itself, do not multiply the base by the exponent).
* **Work:**
  * $4^2 = 4 \times 4 = 16 \neq 8$. Thus, **Choice A** with **`16`**.

### **Question 15**
* **Problem:** Decide whether the expression is positive, negative, or $0$: $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{-4}$.
* **Answer:** **`Choice B: positive`**
* **The Rule:** A negative exponent takes the reciprocal of the base—it never makes a positive base negative: $\left(\frac{1}{a}\right)^{-n} = a^n$.
* **Work:**
  * $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{-4} = 2^4 = 16 > 0$, which is **positive** (**Choice B**).

* **Answer:** **`1/7`**, **`1`**, and **`7`**
