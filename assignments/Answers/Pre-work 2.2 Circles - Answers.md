### **Question 1**
* **Problem:** Fill in the blanks: The set of all points in a plane that are equidistant from a fixed point is a/an (1) _______. The fixed point is called the (2) _______. The distance from this fixed point to any point on the geometric figure is called the (3) _______.
* **Answer:**
  * **(1) circle**
  * **(2) center**
  * **(3) radius**

---

### **Question 2**
* **Problem:** The standard form of the equation of a circle with center $(h,k)$ and radius $r$ is (1) _______.
* **Answer:** **$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$**

---

### **Question 3**
* **Problem:** The circle with equation $x^2 + y^2 = 64$ has center with coordinates _______ and a radius equal to _______.
* **Answer:**
  * Center: **`(0, 0)`**
  * Radius: **`8`** ($\sqrt{64} = 8$)

### **Question 10**
* **Problem:** Match each equation in Column I with its graph in Column II:
  * a. $(x - 4)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 64$
  * b. $(x - 4)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 64$
  * c. $(x + 4)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 64$
  * d. $(x + 4)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 64$
* **Answer:**
  * a. $\to$ **A** (Center $(4, 1)$ in Quadrant I)
  * b. $\to$ **C** (Center $(4, -1)$ in Quadrant IV)
  * c. $\to$ **B** (Center $(-4, 1)$ in Quadrant II)
  * d. $\to$ **D** (Center $(-4, -1)$ in Quadrant III)

### **Question 11**
* **Problem:** How many points lie on the graph of $x^2 + y^2 = 0$?
* **Answer:** **is only one point that lies**
* **Explanation:** Since squares of real numbers are non-negative, $x^2 + y^2 = 0$ is satisfied only by the single point $(0,0)$.

---

### **Question 12**
* **Problem:** How many points lie on the graph of $x^2 + y^2 = -289$?
* **Answer:** **are no points that lie**
* **Explanation:** The sum of two squares of real numbers can never be negative.
