### **Question 11**
* **Problem:** Write the number in standard form: $\frac{-6 + \sqrt{-54}}{3}$
* **Answer:** **`-2 + i\sqrt{6}`** (or `-2 + \sqrt{6}i`)
* **Explanation:** $\sqrt{-54} = \sqrt{9 \cdot 6 \cdot (-1)} = 3i\sqrt{6}$.  
  $\frac{-6 + 3i\sqrt{6}}{3} = -\frac{6}{3} + \frac{3i\sqrt{6}}{3} = -2 + i\sqrt{6}$.

### **Question 12**

* **Answer:** **`3 - 4i\sqrt{3}`** (or `3 - 4\sqrt{3}i`)

### **Question 13**

### **Question 14**

### **Question 17**
* **Problem:** Show that $2 + i$ is a solution of the equation $x^2 - 4x + 5 = 0$.
* **Answers & Steps:**
  1. Substitute: **`2 + i`** for $x$.
  2. Substitution expression: $0 \stackrel{?}{=} (2+i)^2 - 4(2+i) + 5$
  3. Most useful next step: **B. Expand the right side of the equation.**
  4. Next step (uncombined): $0 \stackrel{?}{=}$ **`4 + 4i + i^2 - 8 - 4i + 5`** (or `4 + 4i - 1 - 8 - 4i + 5`)
  5. Combine like terms: $0 \stackrel{?}{=}$ **`0`**
  6. Conclusion: True statement ($0 = 0$), so $2 + i$ is a solution.

### **Question 18**
* **Problem:** Show that $2 + 3i$ is a solution of the equation $x^2 - 4x + 13 = 0$.
* **Answers & Steps:**
  1. Substitute: **`2 + 3i`** for $x$.
  2. Substitution expression: $0 \stackrel{?}{=} (2+3i)^2 - 4(2+3i) + 13$
  3. Most useful next step: **D. Expand the right side of the equation.**
  4. Next step (uncombined): $0 \stackrel{?}{=}$ **`4 + 12i + 9i^2 - 8 - 12i + 13`** (or `4 + 12i - 9 - 8 - 12i + 13`)
  5. Combine like terms: $0 \stackrel{?}{=}$ **`0`**
  6. Conclusion: True statement ($0 = 0$), so $2 + 3i$ is a solution.

### **Question 19**
* **Problem:** Complex numbers describe current $I$, voltage $E$, and impedance $Z$: $E = IZ$.  
  Given $I = 3 + 6i$ and $Z = 6 + 2i$, solve for $E$.
* **Answer:** **`6 + 42i`**
* **Explanation:** $E = (3 + 6i)(6 + 2i) = 18 + 6i + 36i + 12i^2 = 18 + 42i - 12 = 6 + 42i$.
