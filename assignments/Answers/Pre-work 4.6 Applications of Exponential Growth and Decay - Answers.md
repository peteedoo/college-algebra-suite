### **Question 1**
* **Problem:** Select the answers that best complete the given statement: Consider the model for exponential growth or decay given by $A = A_0 e^{kt}$. If $k$ **(1)** ____, the function models the amount, or size, of a growing entity. If $k$ **(2)** ____, the function models the amount, or size, of a decaying entity.
* **Answer:** **(1) `> 0` (second radio button), (2) `< 0` (first radio button)**
* **Graph Equation:** `f(x) = e^(0.5*x)`
* **The Rule:** In the continuous exponential model $A = A_0 e^{kt}$ (with $A_0 > 0$), $k > 0$ represents exponential growth and $k < 0$ represents exponential decay.
* **Work:**
  1. When $k > 0$, as $t$ increases, $e^{kt}$ increases without bound, modeling a growing entity.
  2. When $k < 0$, as $t$ increases, $e^{kt}$ decreases toward $0$, modeling a decaying entity.

### **Question 9**
* **Problem:** A population is increasing according to the exponential function $y = 2e^{0.03t}$, where $y$ is in millions and $t$ is the number of years. Match each question in Column I with the correct procedure in Column II to answer the question:
  - (1) How long will it take for the population to quadruple?
  - (2) When will the population reach 4 million?
  - (3) How large will the population be in 4 years?
  - (4) How large will the population be in 3 months?
* **Answer:**
  - **(1) `Solve 2e^(0.03t) = 8.` (1st choice)**
  - **(2) `Solve 2e^(0.03t) = 4.` (4th choice)**
  - **(3) `Evaluate y = 2e^(0.03(4)).` (3rd choice)**
  - **(4) `Evaluate y = 2e^(0.03(1/4)).` (1st choice)**
* **Graph Equation:** `f(x) = 2 * e^(0.03*x)`
* **The Rule:** Substitute known time $t$ (in years) to evaluate population $y$, or substitute target population $y$ to solve for time $t$.
* **Work:**
  1. **Quadruple:** The initial population at $t = 0$ is $y_0 = 2$ million. Quadrupling means $y = 4 \cdot 2 = 8$, so we solve $2e^{0.03t} = 8$.
  2. **Reach 4 million:** Set $y = 4$ and solve $2e^{0.03t} = 4$.
  3. **In 4 years:** Substitute $t = 4$ and evaluate $y = 2e^{0.03(4)}$.
  4. **In 3 months:** Convert $3\text{ months} = \frac{3}{12}\text{ year} = \frac{1}{4}\text{ year}$ and evaluate $y = 2e^{0.03(1/4)}$.

### **Question 10**
* **Problem:** Strontium-90 decays according to the exponential function $y = y_0 e^{-0.0241t}$, where $t$ is time in years. Match each question in Column I with the correct procedure in Column II to answer the question:
  - (1) If the initial amount of strontium-90 is $100\text{ g}$, how much will remain after $5\text{ years}$?
  - (2) If the initial amount of strontium-90 is $100\text{ g}$, how much will remain after $10\text{ years}$?
  - (3) What is the half-life of strontium-90?
  - (4) How long will it take for any amount of strontium-90 to decay to $80\%$ of its initial amount?
* **Answer:**
  - **(1) `Evaluate y = 100e^(-0.0241(5)).` (1st choice)**
  - **(2) `Evaluate y = 100e^(-0.0241(10)).` (4th choice)**
  - **(3) `Solve (1/2)y_0 = y_0 e^(-0.0241t).` (2nd choice)**
  - **(4) `Solve 0.8y_0 = y_0 e^(-0.0241t).` (4th choice)**
* **Graph Equation:** `f(x) = 100 * e^(-0.0241*x)`
* **The Rule:** Substitute $y_0$ and $t$ to evaluate remaining mass $y$, or set $y$ equal to a fraction of $y_0$ to solve for time $t$.
* **Work:**
  1. **After 5 years ($y_0 = 100$):** Substitute $y_0 = 100$ and $t = 5$ to evaluate $y = 100e^{-0.0241(5)}$.
  2. **After 10 years ($y_0 = 100$):** Substitute $y_0 = 100$ and $t = 10$ to evaluate $y = 100e^{-0.0241(10)}$.
  3. **Half-life:** Set $y = \frac{1}{2}y_0$ and solve $\frac{1}{2}y_0 = y_0 e^{-0.0241t}$.
  4. **Decay to $80\%$:** Set $y = 0.8y_0$ and solve $0.8y_0 = y_0 e^{-0.0241t}$.
