### **Question 1**
* **Problem:** Solve the system by substitution.
  $$\begin{aligned} 3x + 2y &= -16 \quad &(1) \\ -x + y &= 12 \quad &(2) \end{aligned}$$
* **Answer:** **`(-8, 4)`**
* **Graph Equation:** `3x + 2y = -16; -x + y = 12`
* **The Rule:** Solve one equation for one variable, substitute that expression into the other equation, and solve.
* **Work:**
  1. From equation (2), solve for $y$: $y = x + 12$.
  2. Substitute $y = x + 12$ into equation (1):
     $$3x + 2(x + 12) = -16 \implies 3x + 2x + 24 = -16 \implies 5x = -40 \implies x = -8$$
  3. Find $y$: $y = -8 + 12 = 4$.
  4. Solution set: $\{(-8, 4)\}$.

### **Question 2**
* **Problem:** Solve the system of equations using substitution.
  $$\begin{aligned} 3x + y &= 10 \\ y &= 2x \end{aligned}$$
* **Answer:** **`(2, 4)`**
* **Graph Equation:** `3x + y = 10; y = 2x`
* **The Rule:** Substitute the isolated variable expression directly into the other equation.
* **Work:**
  1. Substitute $y = 2x$ into $3x + y = 10$:
     $$3x + 2x = 10 \implies 5x = 10 \implies x = 2$$
  2. Substitute $x = 2$ into $y = 2x$:
     $$y = 2(2) = 4$$
  3. Solution set: $\{(2, 4)\}$.

### **Question 3**

* **Answer:** **`(-6, 6)`**
* **Graph Equation:** `8x + 6y = -12; -3x + y = 24`
* **The Rule:** Isolate $y$ in the second equation and substitute into the first equation.

     $$8x + 6(3x + 24) = -12 \implies 8x + 18x + 144 = -12 \implies 26x = -156 \implies x = -6$$
  3. Find $y$: $y = 3(-6) + 24 = -18 + 24 = 6$.
  4. Solution set: $\{(-6, 6)\}$.

### **Question 4**
* **Problem:** Solve the system by substitution.
  $$\begin{aligned} 3y &= 5x + 6 \quad &(1) \\ x + y &= 2 \quad &(2) \end{aligned}$$
* **Answer:** **`(0, 2)`**
* **Graph Equation:** `3y = 5x + 6; x + y = 2`
* **The Rule:** Solve equation (2) for $y$ (or $x$) and substitute into equation (1).
* **Work:**
  1. From equation (2): $y = 2 - x$.
  2. Substitute into equation (1):
     $$3(2 - x) = 5x + 6 \implies 6 - 3x = 5x + 6 \implies 8x = 0 \implies x = 0$$
  3. Find $y$: $y = 2 - 0 = 2$.
  4. Solution set: $\{(0, 2)\}$.

### **Question 5**
* **Problem:** Solve by the elimination method.
  $$\begin{aligned} 9x - y &= 31 \\ x + 3y &= 19 \end{aligned}$$
* **Answer:** **`(4, 5)`**
* **Graph Equation:** `9x - y = 31; x + 3y = 19`
* **The Rule:** Multiply one or both equations by constants so that one variable has opposite coefficients, then add the equations.
* **Work:**
  1. Multiply the first equation by $3$:
     $$27x - 3y = 93$$
  2. Add to the second equation ($x + 3y = 19$):
     $$28x = 112 \implies x = 4$$
  3. Substitute $x = 4$ into $x + 3y = 19$:
     $$4 + 3y = 19 \implies 3y = 15 \implies y = 5$$
  4. Solution set: $\{(4, 5)\}$.

### **Question 12**
* **Problem:** Solve the system. State whether the system is inconsistent or has infinitely many solutions. If the system has infinitely many solutions, write the solution set with $y$ arbitrary.
  $$\begin{aligned} 2x - 14y &= 0 \\ -3x + 21y &= 10 \end{aligned}$$
* **Answer:** **Choice C: The system is inconsistent.**

  2. Substitute $x = 7y$ into the second equation:
     $$-3(7y) + 21y = 10 \implies -21y + 21y = 10 \implies 0 = 10 \quad \text{(False)}$$
  3. Thus, the system is inconsistent (**Choice C**).

### **Question 13**
* **Problem:** Solve.
  $$\begin{aligned} x + y + z &= -6 \quad &(1) \\ 2x + y - z &= -5 \quad &(2) \\ x - y + z &= -10 \quad &(3) \end{aligned}$$
  The solution set is $\{(\text{____}, \text{____}, \text{____})\}$.
* **Answer:** **`{(-5, 2, -3)}`** (Boxes: `-5`, `2`, `-3`)
* **The Rule:** Eliminate one variable from pairs of equations to reduce the $3 \times 3$ system to a $2 \times 2$ system.
* **Work:**
  1. Subtract equation (3) from equation (1):
     $$(x + y + z) - (x - y + z) = -6 - (-10) \implies 2y = 4 \implies y = 2$$
  2. Add equation (1) and equation (2):
     $$(x + y + z) + (2x + y - z) = -6 + (-5) \implies 3x + 2y = -11$$
  3. Substitute $y = 2$ into $3x + 2y = -11$:
     $$3x + 2(2) = -11 \implies 3x = -15 \implies x = -5$$
  4. Substitute $x = -5, y = 2$ into equation (1):
     $$-5 + 2 + z = -6 \implies z = -3$$

### **Question 14**
* **Problem:** Solve the system.
  $$\begin{aligned} x + 3y + 4z &= 28 \quad &(1) \\ 2x - 3y + 2z &= 20 \quad &(2) \\ 3x - y + z &= 18 \quad &(3) \end{aligned}$$
  The solution set is $\{(\text{____}, \text{____}, \text{____})\}$.
* **Answer:** **`{(4, 0, 6)}`** (Boxes: `4`, `0`, `6`)
* **The Rule:** Eliminate $y$ or $z$ between two pairs of equations, solve the resulting $2 \times 2$ system, and back-substitute.
* **Work:**
  1. Add equations (1) and (2) to eliminate $y$:
     $$3x + 6z = 48 \implies x + 2z = 16 \quad (4)$$
  2. Multiply equation (3) by $3$ and add to equation (1) to eliminate $y$:
     $$3(3x - y + z) + (x + 3y + 4z) = 3(18) + 28 \implies 10x + 7z = 82 \quad (5)$$
  3. From (4), $x = 16 - 2z$. Substitute into (5):
     $$10(16 - 2z) + 7z = 82 \implies 160 - 13z = 82 \implies 13z = 78 \implies z = 6$$
  4. Then $x = 16 - 2(6) = 4$, and from (3), $3(4) - y + 6 = 18 \implies y = 0$.

  $$\begin{aligned} 4x - 2y + 5z &= 49 \quad &(1) \\ 3x - 2y - 2z &= -20 \quad &(2) \\ x - y + 3z &= 23 \quad &(3) \end{aligned}$$

* **Answer:** **`{(6, 10, 9)}`** (Boxes: `6`, `10`, `9`)

  3. Substitute $x = 69 - 7z$ from (4) into (5):
     $$2(69 - 7z) - z = 3 \implies 138 - 15z = 3 \implies 15z = 135 \implies z = 9$$
  4. Substitute $z = 9$ into (4): $x + 63 = 69 \implies x = 6$.
  5. Substitute $x = 6, z = 9$ into (3): $6 - y + 27 = 23 \implies y = 10$.

### **Question 16**
* **Problem:** Solve the system.
  $$\begin{aligned} 2x + 6y - z &= 6 \quad &(1) \\ 3x - 3y + 5z &= -6 \quad &(2) \\ 7x + 9y - 2z &= 11 \quad &(3) \end{aligned}$$

* **The Rule:** Eliminate $z$ from two pairs of equations to solve for $x$ and $y$.
* **Work:**

### **Question 18**
* **Problem:** The manager of a small company that produces roof tile has determined that the total cost in dollars, $C(x)$, of producing $x$ units of tile is given by $C(x) = 200x + 750$, while the revenue in dollars, $R(x)$, from the sale of $x$ units of tile is given by $R(x) = 230x$. Find the break-even point and the cost and revenue at the break-even point.
* **Answer:**
  * The break-even point is **`25`** units.
  * The cost at the break-even point is $**`5750`**.
  * The revenue at the break-even point is $**`5750`**.
* **Graph Equation:** `y = 200x + 750; y = 230x`
* **The Rule:** The break-even point occurs where Revenue equals Cost: $R(x) = C(x)$.
* **Work:**
  1. Set $R(x) = C(x)$:
     $$230x = 200x + 750 \implies 30x = 750 \implies x = 25\text{ units}$$
  2. Evaluate cost and revenue at $x = 25$:
     $$R(25) = 230(25) = 5750$$
     $$C(25) = 200(25) + 750 = 5000 + 750 = 5750$$

### **Question 22**
* **Problem:** A company that manufactures small canoes has a fixed cost of $\$18,000$. It costs $\$20$ to produce each canoe. The selling price is $\$80$ per canoe. (Let $x$ represent the number of canoes produced and sold.)
  * **a.** Write the cost function.
  * **b.** Write the revenue function.
  * **c.** Determine the break-even point and what it means.
* **Answer:**
  * **a.** $C(x) =$ **`18000 + 20x`** (or `20x + 18000`)
  * **b.** $R(x) =$ **`80x`**
  * **c.** Break-even point: **`(300, 24000)`**
  * **Meaning:** **Choice B: the money coming in equals the money going out.**
* **Graph Equation:** `y = 18000 + 20x; y = 80x`
* **The Rule:** $C(x) = \text{fixed cost} + (\text{cost per unit})x$, $R(x) = (\text{price per unit})x$, and break-even is the ordered pair $(x, R(x))$ where $R(x) = C(x)$.
* **Work:**
  1. Cost function: $C(x) = 18000 + 20x$.
  2. Revenue function: $R(x) = 80x$.
  3. Set $R(x) = C(x)$:
     $$80x = 18000 + 20x \implies 60x = 18000 \implies x = 300$$
  4. Find $y$: $R(300) = 80(300) = 24000$. Ordered pair without commas in coordinates: `(300, 24000)`.
  5. At break-even, revenue (money coming in) equals cost (money going out) (**Choice B**).

### **Question 23**
* **Problem:** You invest in a new play. The cost includes an overhead of $\$36,250$, plus production costs of $\$2500$ per performance. A sold-out performance brings in $\$3125$. (Let $x$ represent the number of sold-out performances.)
  * **a.** Write the cost function, $C$.
  * **b.** Write the revenue function, $R$.
  * **c.** Determine the break-even point. Describe what this means.
* **Answer:**
  * **a.** $C(x) =$ **`36250 + 2500x`** (or `2500x + 36250`)
  * **b.** $R(x) =$ **`3125x`**
  * **c.** Break-even point: **`(58, 181250)`**
  * **Describe what this means:** **Choice C: The point where the cost and revenue are equal.**
* **Graph Equation:** `y = 36250 + 2500x; y = 3125x`
* **The Rule:** Equate total cost $C(x)$ and total revenue $R(x)$ to find the break-even point $(x, y)$.
* **Work:**
  1. Cost function: $C(x) = 36250 + 2500x$.
  2. Revenue function: $R(x) = 3125x$.
  3. Solve $R(x) = C(x)$:
     $$3125x = 36250 + 2500x \implies 625x = 36250 \implies x = 58$$
  4. Calculate dollar amount at $x = 58$:
     $$R(58) = 3125(58) = 181250$$
  5. Break-even ordered pair (no commas inside individual coordinates): `(58, 181250)`. This is the point where cost and revenue are equal (**Choice C**).
