### **Question 1**
* **Problem:** The quadratic function $f(x) = a(x - h)^2 + k, a \neq 0$ is in (1) _______ form. The graph of $f$ is called a (2) _______ whose vertex is the point (3) _______. The graph opens upward if (4) _______ and opens downward if (5) _______.
* **Answer:**
  * (1) **vertex**
  * (2) **parabola**
  * (3) **(h,k)**
  * (4) **a > 0**
  * (5) **a < 0**
* **The Rule:** The vertex form $f(x) = a(x - h)^2 + k$ has vertex at $(h, k)$. If $a > 0$, the parabola opens upward with a minimum; if $a < 0$, it opens downward with a maximum.
* **Work:** The standard definitions of quadratic vertex form directly yield parts (1) through (5).

### **Question 8**
* **Problem:** "No quadratic functions have a range of $(-\infty, \infty)$." Determine whether the statement is true or false.
* **Answer:** **A. The statement is true.**
* **The Rule:** Every quadratic function has a vertex that acts as an absolute minimum ($a > 0$, range $[k, \infty)$) or absolute maximum ($a < 0$, range $(-\infty, k]$). Therefore, the range is always bounded on one side and can never be all real numbers.
* **Work:** Since parabolas always have an upper or lower bound at the vertex, no quadratic function can have range $(-\infty, \infty)$ (**Choice A**).

### **Question 25**
* **Problem:** Match each equation with its solution(s):
  * (1) $x^2 = 25$
  * (2) $x^2 = -25$
  * (3) $x^2 + 5 = 0$
  * (4) $x^2 - 5 = 0$
  * (5) $x^2 = -80$
  * (6) $x^2 = 80$
  * (7) $x - 5 = 0$
  * (8) $x + 5 = 0$
* **Answer:**
  * (1) $x^2 = 25 \implies$ **$\pm 5$**
  * (2) $x^2 = -25 \implies$ **$\pm 5i$**
  * (3) $x^2 + 5 = 0 \implies$ **$\pm i\sqrt{5}$**
  * (4) $x^2 - 5 = 0 \implies$ **$\pm\sqrt{5}$**
  * (5) $x^2 = -80 \implies$ **$\pm 4i\sqrt{5}$**
  * (6) $x^2 = 80 \implies$ **$\pm 4\sqrt{5}$**
  * (7) $x - 5 = 0 \implies$ **$5$**
  * (8) $x + 5 = 0 \implies$ **$-5$**
* **The Rule:** Square Root Property: $x^2 = k \implies x = \pm\sqrt{k}$. If $k < 0$, $\sqrt{k} = i\sqrt{|k|}$.
* **Work:**
  1. $\sqrt{25} = 5 \implies \pm 5$
  2. $\sqrt{-25} = 5i \implies \pm 5i$
  3. $x^2 = -5 \implies x = \pm i\sqrt{5}$
  4. $x^2 = 5 \implies x = \pm\sqrt{5}$
  5. $x^2 = -80 \implies x = \pm\sqrt{-80} = \pm\sqrt{16 \cdot 5 \cdot (-1)} = \pm 4i\sqrt{5}$
  6. $x^2 = 80 \implies x = \pm\sqrt{80} = \pm 4\sqrt{5}$
  7. $x - 5 = 0 \implies x = 5$
  8. $x + 5 = 0 \implies x = -5$

### **Question 30**
* **Problem:** Solve by completing the square: $-2x^2 + 4x + 3 = 0$.
* **Answer:** **`\frac{2 - \sqrt{10}}{2}, \frac{2 + \sqrt{10}}{2}`** (or `1 - \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}, 1 + \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}`)

  $$x - 1 = \pm\sqrt{\frac{5}{2}} = \pm\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2} \implies x = 1 \pm\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2} = \frac{2 \pm\sqrt{10}}{2}$$

### **Question 44**
* **Problem:** A farmer has $450$ feet of fencing to enclose a rectangular plot bordering a river (sides $x$, $x$, and $450 - 2x$). Find the width, length, and largest area.
* **Answer:**
  * Width $x$: **`112.5`** feet
  * Length $450 - 2x$: **`225`** feet
  * Largest area: **`25312.5`** square feet
* **Graph Equation:** `A(x) = -2*x^2 + 450*x`
* **The Rule:** Area is $A(x) = x(450 - 2x) = -2x^2 + 450x$. Maximum occurs at vertex $x = -\frac{b}{2a}$.
* **Work:**
  * Width: $x = -\frac{450}{2(-2)} = \frac{450}{4} = 112.5$ feet.
  * Length: $450 - 2(112.5) = 450 - 225 = 225$ feet.
  * Maximum area: $A(112.5) = 112.5 \times 225 = 25,312.5$ sq ft.
