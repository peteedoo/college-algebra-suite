### **Question 1**
* **Problem:** Find an approximate irrational solution to $2^x = 3$. (Round to four decimal places.)
* **Answer:** **Choice A: The solution set is `{1.5850}`**
* **Graph Equation:** `f(x) = 2^x - 3`
* **The Rule:** Take the natural logarithm of both sides and apply the power rule $\ln(a^x) = x\ln(a)$.
* **Work:**
  1. Take the natural logarithm of both sides: $\ln(2^x) = \ln(3)$
  2. Bring down the exponent: $x \ln(2) = \ln(3)$
  3. Divide by $\ln(2)$: $x = \frac{\ln(3)}{\ln(2)} \approx \frac{1.09861229}{0.69314718} \approx 1.58496 \approx 1.5850$

### **Question 4**
* **Problem:** Solve the exponential equation $2^{x-1} = 3^{2x}$. Express irrational solutions as decimals correct to the nearest thousandth.
* **Answer:** **Choice A: The solution set is `{-0.461}`**
* **Graph Equation:** `f(x) = 2^(x-1) - 3^(2*x)`
* **The Rule:** Take the natural logarithm of both sides, distribute, and group all $x$-terms on one side.
* **Work:**
  1. Take the natural logarithm of both sides: $\ln(2^{x-1}) = \ln(3^{2x})$
  2. Bring down the exponents: $(x - 1)\ln(2) = 2x\ln(3)$
  3. Expand and group $x$-terms: $x\ln(2) - \ln(2) = 2x\ln(3) \implies x(\ln(2) - 2\ln(3)) = \ln(2)$
  4. Divide to solve for $x$: $x = \frac{\ln(2)}{\ln(2) - 2\ln(3)} \approx \frac{0.69314718}{0.69314718 - 2.19722458} \approx \frac{0.69314718}{-1.50407740} \approx -0.460845 \approx -0.461$

* **Work:**

  4. Collect $x$-terms on the right and constants on the left: $3\ln(6) + \ln(4) = x(2\ln(4) - \ln(6))$
  5. Solve for $x$: $x = \frac{3\ln(6) + \ln(4)}{2\ln(4) - \ln(6)} \approx \frac{5.37527841 + 1.38629436}{2.77258872 - 1.79175947} = \frac{6.76157277}{0.98082925} \approx 6.89373 \approx 6.894$

  2. Factor the quadratic: $(u - 4)(u - 8) = 0 \implies u = 4 \text{ or } u = 8$
  3. Substitute back $e^x = u$:
     - $e^x = 4 \implies x = \ln 4$
     - $e^x = 8 \implies x = \ln 8$

### **Question 30**
* **Problem:** How much time will be needed for $\$38,000$ to grow to $\$40,331.81$ if deposited at $6\%$ compounded quarterly? Use the formula $A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{k}\right)^{kt}$ and round to the nearest tenth as needed.
* **Answer:** **`1` year (or `1.0` years)**
* **Graph Equation:** `f(x) = 38000 * (1 + 0.06/4)^(4*x) - 40331.81`
* **The Rule:** Substitute $A$, $P$, $r$, and $k$ into the compound interest formula, isolate the exponential factor, and take the natural logarithm of both sides.
* **Work:**
  1. Substitute $A = 40331.81$, $P = 38000$, $r = 0.06$, and $k = 4$:
     $$40331.81 = 38000\left(1 + \frac{0.06}{4}\right)^{4t} = 38000(1.015)^{4t}$$
  2. Divide both sides by $38000$:
     $$(1.015)^{4t} = \frac{40331.81}{38000} \approx 1.06136342$$
  3. Take the natural logarithm of both sides:
     $$4t \ln(1.015) = \ln\left(\frac{40331.81}{38000}\right)$$
  4. Solve for $t$:
     $$t = \frac{\ln(40331.81 / 38000)}{4 \ln(1.015)} \approx \frac{0.05955436}{0.05955448} \approx 1.0\text{ year}$$
