### **Question 16**
* **Problem:** Give the center and radius of $x^2 + y^2 + 10x + 6y + 30 = 0$.
* **Answers:**
  * **Center:** **`(-5, -3)`**
  * **Radius:** **`2`**
* **Explanation:**  
  $(x^2 + 10x + 25) + (y^2 + 6y + 9) = -30 + 25 + 9$  
  $(x + 5)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 4 \implies r = \sqrt{4} = 2$.

* **Answers:**
  * **Center:** **`(6, -2)`**
  * **Radius:** **`5`**
* **Explanation:**  
  $(x^2 - 12x + 36) + (y^2 + 4y + 4) = -15 + 36 + 4$  
  $(x - 6)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 25 \implies r = 5$.

### **Question 18**

### **Question 19**
* **Problem:** Decide whether or not $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 6y + 14 = 0$ has a circle as its graph.
* **Answers:** **D. The graph is nonexistent.**
* **Explanation:**

### **Question 21**
* **Problem:** Give the center and radius of $4x^2 + 4x + 4y^2 - 32y - 79 = 0$.
* **Answers:**
  * **Center:** **`(-1/2, 4)`** (or `(-0.5, 4)`)
  * **Radius:** **`6`**
* **Explanation:**  
  Divide by 4: $x^2 + x + y^2 - 8y = \frac{79}{4}$  
  $(x^2 + x + \frac{1}{4}) + (y^2 - 8y + 16) = \frac{79}{4} + \frac{1}{4} + 16 = \frac{80}{4} + 16 = 20 + 16 = 36$  
  $(x + \frac{1}{2})^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 36 \implies r = \sqrt{36} = 6$.
