### **Question 1**
* **Problem:** Fill in the blank to correctly complete the sentence. The real solutions of $f(x) < 0$ are the $x$-values for which the graph lies (1) ________ the $x$-axis.
* **Answer:** **(1) `below`**
* **The Rule:** Points on the graph of $y = f(x)$ with $y < 0$ lie strictly below the $x$-axis.
* **Work:**
  1. Since $y = f(x)$, the inequality $f(x) < 0$ means $y < 0$.
  2. Points with negative $y$-coordinates lie **below** the $x$-axis.

### **Question 3**
* **Problem:** Use the graph to solve the equation: $4x(x - 2)(x - 3) = 0$
* **Answer:** **`0, 2, 3`** (Solution set: `{0, 2, 3}`)
* **Graph Equation:** `f(x) = 4x*(x - 2)*(x - 3)`
* **The Rule:** The real solutions to $f(x) = 0$ are the $x$-intercepts of the graph of $f(x)$.
* **Work:**
  1. Set each factor equal to zero (or read the $x$-intercepts directly from the graph):
     $$4x = 0 \implies x = 0$$
     $$x - 2 = 0 \implies x = 2$$
     $$x - 3 = 0 \implies x = 3$$
  2. The solution set is **`{0, 2, 3}`**.

### **Question 4**
* **Problem:** Fill in each blank so that the resulting statement is true. In interval notation, $[3, 9)$ represents the set of real numbers between (1) ________ and (2) ________ including (3) ________ but not (4) ________.
* **Answer:** **(1) `3`, (2) `9,`, (3) `3`, (4) `9.`**
* **The Rule:** A square bracket `[` or `]` includes the endpoint, while a parenthesis `(` or `)` excludes the endpoint.
* **Work:**
  1. The interval $[3, 9)$ contains all real numbers between **$3$** and **$9$**.
  2. Because $3$ has a bracket `[`, it is **included**; because $9$ has a parenthesis `)`, it is **not** included.

### **Question 5**
* **Problem:** Fill in the blank so that the resulting statement is true. In interval notation, $(-2, \infty)$ represents the set of real numbers (1) ________ $-2$.
* **Answer:** **(1) `greater than`**
* **The Rule:** An open interval $(a, \infty)$ represents all real numbers strictly greater than $a$ ($x > a$).
* **Work:**
  1. The parenthesis at $-2$ excludes $-2$, and $\infty$ extends to all larger numbers.
  2. Thus, $(-2, \infty)$ represents all real numbers **greater than** $-2$.

### **Question 6**
* **Problem:** Fill in the blank so that the resulting statement is true. In interval notation, $(-\infty, -1]$ represents the set of real numbers (1) ________ $-1$.
* **Answer:** **(1) `less than or equal to`**
* **The Rule:** A half-open interval $(-\infty, b]$ represents all real numbers less than or equal to $b$ ($x \le b$).
* **Work:**
  1. Extending from $-\infty$ up to $-1$ with a square bracket `]` means $x \le -1$.
  2. Thus, $(-\infty, -1]$ represents the set of real numbers **less than or equal to** $-1$.

### **Question 7**
* **Problem:** Use the graph to solve the inequality: $7x(x - 2)(x - 3) < 0$
* **Answer:** `(-\infty, 0) \cup (2, 3)`
* **Graph Equation:** `7x*(x - 2)*(x - 3) < 0`
* **The Rule:** For $f(x) < 0$, identify the open intervals of $x$ where the graph lies strictly below the $x$-axis.
* **Work:**
  1. The $x$-intercepts (zeros) are $x = 0, 2, 3$.
  2. From the graph, $f(x)$ lies below the $x$-axis to the left of $0$ and between $2$ and $3$.
  3. Because the inequality is strict ($<$), exclude the endpoints: **`(-\infty, 0) \cup (2, 3)`**.

* **Answer:** `(0, 1) \cup (3, \infty)`
* **Graph Equation:** `5x*(x - 1)*(x - 3) > 0`

* **Work:**
  1. The $x$-intercepts are $x = 0, 1, 3$.
  2. From the graph, $f(x)$ lies above the $x$-axis between $0$ and $1$, and to the right of $3$.
  3. Because the inequality is strict ($>$), exclude the endpoints: **`(0, 1) \cup (3, \infty)`**.

### **Question 9**
* **Problem:** Use the graph to solve the inequality: $7x(x - 1)(x - 2) \ge 0$
* **Answer:** `[0, 1] \cup [2, \infty)`
* **Graph Equation:** `7x*(x - 1)*(x - 2) >= 0`
* **The Rule:** For $f(x) \ge 0$, identify all intervals where the graph is on or above the $x$-axis, using square brackets at the zeros.
* **Work:**
  1. The $x$-intercepts are $x = 0, 1, 2$.
  2. The graph is above or on the $x$-axis from $0$ to $1$ and from $2$ to $\infty$.
  3. Including the zeros gives **`[0, 1] \cup [2, \infty)`**.

### **Question 10**
* **Problem:** Use the graph to solve the equation: $\frac{2(x - 3)}{(x - 1)(x - 4)} = 0$
* **Answer:** **`3`** (Solution set: `{3}`)
* **Graph Equation:** `f(x) = (2*(x - 3))/((x - 1)*(x - 4))`
* **The Rule:** A rational function equals $0$ where its numerator equals $0$ (and its denominator is nonzero), which corresponds to the $x$-intercept of the graph.
* **Work:**
  1. Set the numerator equal to zero:
     $$2(x - 3) = 0 \implies x = 3$$
  2. The graph crosses the $x$-axis at $(3, 0)$, so the solution set is **`{3}`**.

### **Question 11**
* **Problem:** Use the graph to solve the inequality: $\frac{3(x - 2)}{(x - 1)(x - 3)} < 0$
* **Answer:** `(-\infty, 1) \cup (2, 3)`
* **Graph Equation:** `(3*(x - 2))/((x - 1)*(x - 3)) < 0`
* **The Rule:** Find the $x$-intervals where the rational function graph lies strictly below the $x$-axis, bounded by vertical asymptotes and $x$-intercepts.
* **Work:**
  1. Boundary values are the vertical asymptotes $x = 1, 3$ and the $x$-intercept $x = 2$.
  2. From the graph, the curve is below the $x$-axis on $(-\infty, 1)$ and on $(2, 3)$.
  3. The solution set is **`(-\infty, 1) \cup (2, 3)`**.

### **Question 13**
* **Problem:** Use the graph to solve the inequality: $\frac{2(x - 2)}{(x - 1)(x - 4)} \le 0$
* **Answer:** `(-\infty, 1) \cup [2, 4)`
* **Graph Equation:** `(2*(x - 2))/((x - 1)*(x - 4)) <= 0`
* **The Rule:** For $\le 0$, include the $x$-intercepts (where $f(x) = 0$) with square brackets, but **always exclude** vertical asymptotes (where $f(x)$ is undefined) with parentheses.
* **Work:**
  1. Vertical asymptotes are at $x = 1$ and $x = 4$ (must be excluded with parentheses).
  2. The $x$-intercept is at $x = 2$ (included with a bracket `[` because of $\le 0$).
  3. From the graph, $f(x) \le 0$ on **`(-\infty, 1) \cup [2, 4)`**.

### **Question 14**
* **Problem:** Select the correct choice that completes the sentence below. If three negative numbers are multiplied, the product is (1) ________.
* **Answer:** **(1) `negative.`**
* **The Rule:** The product of an odd number of negative factors is always negative; the product of an even number of negative factors is positive.
* **Work:**
  1. Multiply the signs of three negative numbers:
     $$(-) \cdot (-) \cdot (-) = (+) \cdot (-) = (-)$$
  2. Thus, the product is **negative**.

### **Question 15**
* **Problem:** Which of the given inequalities has the solution set $(-\infty, \infty)$?
  * A. $(8x + 7)^2 < 0$
  * B. $(x - 3)^2 \ge 0$
  * C. $(6x - 4)^2 > 0$
  * D. $(5x - 6)^2 \le 0$
* **Answer:** **Choice B (`(x - 3)^2 >= 0`)**
* **Graph Equation:** `(x - 3)^2 >= 0`
* **The Rule:** The square of any real expression is always greater than or equal to zero ($A^2 \ge 0$ for all real $x$).
* **Work:**
  1. For any real number $x$, $(x - 3)^2$ is either positive (when $x \neq 3$) or $0$ (when $x = 3$).
  2. Therefore, $(x - 3)^2 \ge 0$ holds for every real number $x \in (-\infty, \infty)$.
